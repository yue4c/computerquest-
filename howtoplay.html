HOW TO PLAY

Welcome, System Trainee! Follow these steps to complete your mission
and restore the computer system.

◎

1. READ THE MISSION

Each level gives you a mission or a question. Read it carefully before you start.

☝️

2. CLICK TO INTERACT

Click on buttons, objects or options to explore, answer questions or collect items.

🏅

3. EARN XP

Answer correctly and complete challenges to earn XP and unlock new areas.

❤️❤️❤️

4. WATCH YOUR LIVES

You have 3 lives. Wrong answers may cost you a life!

🔒

5. UNLOCK & PROGRESS

Complete T01 to unlock T02, then complete T02 to unlock T03 and the final challenge.

🏆

6. RESTORE THE SYSTEM

Finish all challenges, solve the cases and become a COMPUTER ARCHITECT!

💡
NEED A HINT?

Use the HINT button during challenging levels.

💬
EXPLORE EVERYTHING

Click around! You might find useful information.
